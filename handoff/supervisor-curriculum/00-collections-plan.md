# Plan: organizing all the material into collections, with a space for supervisors and managers

Date: October 31, 2026. This is the plan the build follows. The application work is reported separately.

## The short version

Everything goes in, and nothing is locked. Each course gets a home in a small set of shelves, so a reader no longer faces a flat list of 694.

- **Staff see 8 collections**, named by subject.
- **Supervisors and managers get a space of their own**, opening with one question: "What are you deciding?" It has 9 collections, each a decision. Under them sit 89 plain questions, and each question names one course to start with.
- **Four suggested paths** sit beside that: first-year supervisors, experienced supervisors, managers of managers, and senior leaders. All are optional.
- **No sign-in, no team view, no assignments, no scores.** All of it is public. Progress stays in the reader's own browser, using a feature the app already has.
- **A course can sit on both sides.** 238 items do. The supervisor space is a door for a role's decisions, and anyone can walk through it.
- **One course is required.** The Equity Analysis Toolkit (`dhs-equity-analysis-toolkit`) is required of all DHS staff, regardless of status. It is listed first in "Start here: equity at DSD", as well as in "Policy, data and public decisions", and it is marked "Required of all DHS staff" wherever it appears in these collections.

The companion file, `collections-placement.csv`, is the complete table. It has one row for each of the 622 served courses, 72 self-directed courses and 19 new `sm-` courses, plus 4 rows for the Tribal and Native referral-only courses, 717 rows in all.

The Toolkit sits second on the first-year path, after the first-week course, and first on the other three, because it is required of all DHS staff whichever path a reader follows. The times come from the app's own stated course lengths, using the middle of any range. I have not tested them against real readers. They count courses only, not optional practice or live sessions. Where a new course's draft existed when this table was built (19 of 19), its minutes are the sum of its lessons. The rest use a planned 60 minutes.

## How the earlier report's starting points changed

- **All 34 primary courses from the earlier report are still primary**, and all 34 appear in the supervisor space. The new courses fill the gaps beside them. They did not displace any of the 34.
- **The earlier report's holds are gone.** These now sit in collections with no gate and no label:
  - `dc-l51-affirmative-action-in-state-employment` is the starting course on state hiring rules, and it is on the first-year path.
  - `div-f01-white-privilege` is the starting course on workplace norms. `div-a02-whiteness-institutional-norm` and `div-a11-white-supremacy-culture` sit beside it. All three are on the experienced path and in the staff view.
  - The hiring module is in full: `sm-hiring-decisions`, `dc-l54`, `dc-l53`, `dc-l52`, `screening-and-selection`, `sourcing-and-outreach`, `job-design-and-the-posting`, and self-directed I17.
- **Removed:** counsel review items, approval steps, the separate state survey and measurement plan, and the "required training" framing. The app is voluntary, with the one exception of the Equity Analysis Toolkit, and it does not satisfy Minn. Stat. 43A.21; the state's own training does that.
- **The IDI continuum courses are in** as ordinary learning. They teach the idea and are not the IDI instrument. The app's promise stands: no IDI or CQ result goes into any review or selection.
