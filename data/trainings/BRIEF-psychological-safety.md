## Lessons from the Cultural Humility reviews (apply from the start)

1. **Option lengths:** every option in every decision and check is 20 to 35 words; the strong option is never the
   longest; at least one weak option is a tempting near-miss with a believable reason. No course catch-phrases in
   the strong option that give it away.
2. **Branch continuity:** every consequence must fit every path that leads into the next decision. Recovery
   decisions stay in the same moment; never jump ahead ("a week later") on a path that then returns to the moment.
   Never refer to something that only happened on another path.
3. **No invented DHS rules:** a deadline, legal standard, procedure, threshold or unit name that is not in the
   readings is either clearly this scenario's own fact ("this team's practice") or routed to "the person or area
   responsible". Reporting duties must match the readings exactly (for example, what a supervisor must do with a
   harassment or discrimination concern, as the readings state it).
4. **Coaching, not grading;** never declare a repair "complete" or "full".
5. **Privacy:** a strong choice never shares a person's private reason without asking them first.
6. **Psychological safety is not comfort or niceness:** keep the readings' distinction between safety and low
   standards or avoiding hard feedback, and between safety and harassment/conduct processes that still apply.
7. **No names; roles and job classifications only; the cast simulates DHS employees** (owner, 2026-11-15). The
   cast must never be presented as speaking for the agency.
