DHS workforce and job competencies
Research brief for the One DHS People, Access and Culture rebuild. Prepared November 13, 2026, from public sources only.
What I found, in short
Today&apos;s DHS is about 2,400 people. MMB&apos;s January 2026 compensation report lists 2,285 DHS employees, the budget book shows roughly 1,950 to 2,120 full-time equivalents for fiscal years 2026 and 2027, and Gary confirmed on November 13, 2026 that roughly 2,400 is the right planning number. Before the split, DHS had about 7,500.
The MAPE contract does not contain competencies. Contracts set pay, hours, discipline and seniority. Competencies live in two other places: MMB&apos;s class specifications (job-specific knowledge, skills and abilities) and MMB&apos;s enterprise leader competency model.
Minnesota has one enterprise competency model, not four. It has five competencies plus Leading with DEAI, and it grades behavior as in-progress, proficient or expert. I found no separate published sets for individual contributors, supervisors, managers and executives.
The platform is voluntary, but some of what it teaches is not. The DHS Equity Policy requires annual equity learning and Equity Analysis Toolkit use from every employee. The app can be the voluntary place where people get good at things the policy already expects of them.
Role relevance can be built on three public anchors: the employee&apos;s bargaining unit or plan (which signals level), their classification series (which signals the kind of work), and their administration (which signals the population served).
How to read the labels: Verified. means I read it in a linked public source. Inferred. means it is my reasoning or general knowledge of Minnesota state government, without a source I could open. Not confirmed. means I looked and could not confirm it.

5. Competency frameworks
The enterprise model
Verified. MMB&apos;s Competency Definition Guide (March 2024) sets five leader competencies for existing and emerging leaders, written so any employee can self-assess. Each has in-progress, proficient and expert behaviors. Equity language is built into every one, including cultural humility, anti-racism and naming ableism. Verified. MMB&apos;s training catalog adds a sixth label, Leading with DEAI, with five topics: accessibility, allyship, creating a culture of inclusion, cultural awareness, competence and humility, and pronouns (Leadership Learning Hub, Leading with DEAI). Supervisor Core and Manager Core are tagged to these competencies and are required during probation (MMB Enterprise Talent Development core programs). MMB also publishes sample job qualifications written against the model (MMB Sample Qualifications for Leader Competencies).
Not confirmed. I found no published MMB framework that separates individual contributor, supervisor, manager and executive competencies, and no DHS-specific competency model. If DHS has an internal one, it is not on the public site.
Equity, access and inclusion requirements
Verified. DHS Equity Policy 2.0. Applies to all employees. Every employee must attend annual equity learning, use the Equity Analysis Toolkit, examine personal bias, and sign an annual commitment. A formal equity analysis is required for actions with long-term impact that need leadership approval, including budgets, hiring, contracting, IT modernization and service delivery. Supervisors through the commissioner must promote equity in strategic planning. Each administration must run an Equity Committee, review contracts through an equity lens, do ongoing community engagement and apply the National CLAS Standards. Non-compliance can lead to discipline. Source: DHS Equity Policy, version 2.0 (effective August 4, 2023).
Verified. Digital accessibility. State agencies must meet the state standard, which adopted WCAG 2.1 Level AA on July 1, 2024 and incorporates Section 508 (MNIT Accessibility Policies and Standards). The federal ADA Title II web rule uses the same standard, and its deadline for large public entities was extended in April 2026 to April 26, 2027 (Federal Register, ADA Title II web rule compliance date extension (April 20, 2026)).
Verified. Olmstead. The September 2026 plan covers 13 state entities and eight topics. DHS leads goals on mobile crisis outcomes and on cutting delays in Medicaid and waiver eligibility reviews. The plan treats anti-ableism and disability training as core workforce components and stresses person-centered practice (Minnesota Olmstead Plan, September 2026).
Verified. Language access. DHS runs a Limited English Proficiency program providing spoken and written language help, with a Multilingual Referral Line (DHS Limited English Proficiency program pages). Inferred. The legal basis is Title VI of the Civil Rights Act. I could not open the current DHS plan text.
Verified. Statewide conduct policies. Code of Conduct training within a week of hire, acknowledgment of the Sexual Harassment Prohibited policy, and Workplace Violence Prevention training (Minnesota Department of Administration, required policies and training for state employees).

How I would use it in the app: tag every resource with one or more competencies and a depth, then let each person&apos;s family set the default order of what they see. Nothing is hidden, because the platform is voluntary and people move between families. A supervisor who was a policy specialist last year should still find the policy material.
Three choices I made that you may want to overrule. First, Leading Self is marked practice for everyone, with nobody at deep, because it is the common entry point and should not be ranked by rank. Second, eligibility and direct public service staff carry the deepest access tags (accessibility, disability inclusion, language access), since they are the ones talking to the public. Third, I kept Minnesota IT Services staff out because they are not DHS employees, though they build the systems the accessibility standard applies to. An eleventh family for technology partners would be reasonable.
7. Further thoughts as your thought partner
Voluntary changes the design problem from load to pull. With no one required to attend, the risk is that 650 to 1,000 courses sit unused. Relevance by role is what makes a voluntary catalog feel small enough to start. The matrix is the mechanism for that.
Anchor to things employees already have to do. The Individual Development Plan, the annual appraisal, Supervisor and Manager Core, and the annual equity learning requirement are existing moments when people look for learning. Tagging resources to those moments gives a voluntary platform a reason to be opened.
Use MMB&apos;s language. Tagging to the six enterprise competencies means an employee can carry what they learn into a performance review or a job application elsewhere in state government, and it makes the app easier for DHS leadership to defend.
Scaling from the Disability Services Division to the agency is a change of kind. One division is mostly policy and program professionals. The agency adds investigators, eligibility staff, finance, legal and a real management ladder. Content written for the proof of concept will lean toward family 4, so the first gap analysis should be how many of the 653 courses serve families 5 through 10.
