Host 1: Standard one is the foundation. Host 2: Notice that the focus is the person's experience of the service, not simply whether we sent information. A letter can be accurate and still be difficult to use. An interpreter can be scheduled and still arrive too late for meaningful participation. A form can be online and still be inaccessible to someone who uses assistive technology or does not have reliable internet access.

Host 1: Equity asks us to consider what people need to participate meaningfully. Giving everyone the same format does not tell us whether everyone can use it. At the same time, we should not assume a person's needs from their name, appearance, disability, language or community membership. Ask respectfully, offer choices within the available process, and confirm whether the response works.
