Make room for questions and appropriate escalation. A person who identifies a gap should not have to pretend they already know how to resolve it.

Host 1: Standard four addresses regular education and training. A useful learning opportunity connects to the work staff do and the choices they face. A brief review of a notice or an assistance request can be more useful than repeating a definition without practicing its use.

Host 1: Ask whether the proposed process is dependable when the usual contact is unavailable. Consider workload and capacity.
