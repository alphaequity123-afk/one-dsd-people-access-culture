Welcome to One DSD People, Access and Culture

Welcome to One DSD
Welcome to One DSD People, Access and Culture. Whether you're a director, manager, supervisor, or staff member, this is a place to connect equity with your everyday work. You can build understanding, prepare for a decision, or find a useful resource. Learning here is voluntary. Start where you are, and explore at your own pace.

Find a place to begin
Choose Enter One DSD to reach the home page. Begin with a question from your work, then explore learning, practical tools, or support. You can go directly to a resource, follow a learning path, or return to something you have already started. There is no required order.

Connect learning with work
Connect learning with work helps you choose a starting point: build your understanding, work through a decision, or examine what helped. The program connects learning, practice, and reflection. Choose the starting point that fits your responsibility and return as your question develops.

Connect with disability services
For disability services work, explore DSD programs and services. Find a relevant service, then open Equity question and resources. These connections help you consider the people involved, possible barriers, and useful guidance. DSD sits within the Aging and Disability Services Administration, or ADSA. Keep your own responsibilities and the relevant requirements in view.

Choose useful learning
In Learning and resources, search for a question or choose a theme. Foundation, Intermediate, and Advanced describe learning depth, not your job title. Look for what a course helps you practice and prepare. You can explore team collaboration, policy work, communication, and other topics, choosing what is useful for the work ahead.

Find a resource
The library brings courses, practical guidance, books, and recordings together. Search for a topic or follow a theme. Where a podcast or short reading is available, choose whether to listen or read the accompanying words. You can pause and return to a passage. Listening is optional.

Explore books and ideas
Books and ideas for your work offers two hundred readings in nine collections. Search by title, author, or idea, or choose a reading path. Each reading includes a summary, key ideas, and questions. Compare perspectives, discuss a question, and follow connections into practice. These are authors’ perspectives, not agency policy.

See the wider purpose
To see the wider purpose, open People, Access and Culture in practice from the home page. The first video introduces the Equity Outcome Map. The second follows DSD examples connected to the six ADSA goals. Beside the examples, links show which existing tools can help you take the next step.

Work through a decision
The Equity Analysis Toolkit helps you examine a decision. Consider who is affected, what the evidence supports, and which alternatives deserve attention. Open Practice with the toolkit to watch a worked example, explore the completed responses, and try a separate situation. Use the questions to prepare your own draft for the appropriate review.

Bring in another perspective
Resources can help you prepare thoughtful questions. Minnesota Communities offers context, and the cultural and language-access guidance helps you consider participation. Use them alongside conversations with the people affected. A resource cannot tell you what every person needs, and a prepared analysis does not replace the responsible decision-maker.

Return to your work
My work helps you return to saved resources and plans. Use the same browser and device, and check the saving message on each activity. Download a copy when needed. Keep private client and personnel information out of learning notes. For a question needing an official response, use Find the right person or office.

Begin with one useful step
Begin with one question, one useful resource, and one step you can try. Notice what changed and whose experience still needs attention. Return to this User guide whenever you need directions. The written guide includes links and a download so you can find your way again.
