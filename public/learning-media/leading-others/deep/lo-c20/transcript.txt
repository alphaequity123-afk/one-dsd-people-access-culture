Person-centered decisions: who decides



Lead Agency Case Manager: I have a young man who just turned 18. He wants to move in with a friend and work at a grocery store. His parents want guardianship. Honestly, I worry about him signing a lease he does not understand.

Human Services Supervisor: That is a real worry. Before anyone talks about guardianship, what has he said he wants help with?

Lead Agency Case Manager: I have mostly talked with his mother. She calls, and he is usually at school.

Human Services Supervisor: That happens a lot. The parent is the one on the phone. But under Minnesota law, a court can appoint a guardian only if his needs cannot be met in a less restrictive way. Supported decision making is one of the alternatives named. So his own view is the starting point.

Lead Agency Case Manager: Does supported decision making actually work? I hear a lot of enthusiasm and not many numbers.

Human Services Supervisor: That is a fair challenge. Reviews call the evidence limited. Pilots are small. A 2025 DHS report says early results suggest better well-being, but it is still being studied. What is known is that young people with stronger self-determination are more likely to have jobs and community access a year after high school.

Lead Agency Case Manager: So the case for choice is strong even if the program evidence is thin.

Human Services Supervisor: Yes. And the law is clear. Now, your worry about the lease. What exactly could go wrong?

Lead Agency Case Manager: He might agree to rent he cannot afford, or a lease with terms he does not follow.

Human Services Supervisor: Then ask him who he trusts to look at contracts with him before he signs. An uncle, a friend, a benefits counselor. That addresses the specific risk and leaves the decision with him.

Lead Agency Case Manager: His mother may still want guardianship.

Human Services Supervisor: She may. Listen to what she fears most. Often a fear about money or safety has a specific answer. If she still goes to court, the court has to consider alternatives and explain why they would not work. What he wants, and which supports were tried, will matter.
