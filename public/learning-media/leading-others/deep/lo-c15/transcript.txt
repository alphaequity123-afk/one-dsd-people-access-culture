Leading people through change



Human Services Supervisor: Thanks for walking that case with me. I want to be honest. When you raised the concern the third time, my first instinct was to explain the policy again.

Disability Services Program Consultant: I could tell. And I understand it. But I was not confused about the policy. I was thinking about one person whose plan changes almost every month.

Human Services Supervisor: That is what I missed. You were describing a real case, and I was treating it as an attitude problem.

Disability Services Program Consultant: When we walked it through, most of the new process held up better than I expected. I will say that to the team.

Human Services Supervisor: That will carry more weight from you than from me. And the second change in one month really had no owner. I have sent that to the person responsible for the process. I expect an answer by the end of next week.

Disability Services Program Consultant: What helps me is knowing when. Before, I kept hearing that things would be worked out, with no date.

Human Services Supervisor: That fits what research on change in public agencies has found. In one study of youth care workers, getting more information was not linked to commitment. Taking part in how the change worked was. So was feeling able to handle it.

Disability Services Program Consultant: That matches how I feel. I do not need more emails. I need to know my concerns go somewhere.

Human Services Supervisor: There is also a lot of talk about the new director changing things. I am telling staff and counties the same thing. The start date and the steps are confirmed. The training schedule is still open, and I will update everyone on Friday.

Disability Services Program Consultant: Honestly, saying what you do not know makes me trust the rest more.

Human Services Supervisor: Can I ask you to help with one more thing? Would you bring two more tough cases to next week's meeting? I would rather find the gaps now than have a person waiting for services find them in March.

Disability Services Program Consultant: I can do that. I will bring the hardest ones I have.
