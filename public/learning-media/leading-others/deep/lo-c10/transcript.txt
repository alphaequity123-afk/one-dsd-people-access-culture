Delegating real authority



Human Services Supervisor: I want you to lead the provider staffing guide. You know these providers better than anyone on the team.

Senior Program Consultant: I would like that. Can I ask what leading means here? On my last team, leading meant writing a draft and then waiting for edits on every page.

Human Services Supervisor: Fair question. Let's sort it out now. You decide the structure, the wording and who tests it with you. Legal review stays with the person or area responsible for it. The release date stays with me, because it ties to the rule change.

Senior Program Consultant: And if I want to add a section on common mistakes providers make?

Human Services Supervisor: That is yours. Talk to me first only if a choice changes what providers are required to do. Then it needs a policy check.

Senior Program Consultant: That helps. Honestly, my review queue is already long. I was going to fit this in at night.

Human Services Supervisor: Please don't. Research on empowering leadership finds that the same trust that raises confidence can also raise strain, and strain eats the gain. Which four reviews can move to someone else for six weeks?

Senior Program Consultant: The two day-service reviews and the two renewals that are not due until spring.

Human Services Supervisor: Done. I will move them today. What else do you need to make good decisions on this?

Senior Program Consultant: Time with the policy lead who wrote the rule summary, and the call-line notes on provider questions.

Human Services Supervisor: I will introduce you to the policy lead this week and ask the call-line supervisor to share the notes. Let's check in once, when you have a first draft. Call me sooner if something touches requirements.

Senior Program Consultant: One more thing. If you would have done the structure differently, will you change it?

Human Services Supervisor: I will tell you what I would have done and ask what you weighed. I will only reverse something that puts people served, the law or policy at risk. Otherwise, it stands.
