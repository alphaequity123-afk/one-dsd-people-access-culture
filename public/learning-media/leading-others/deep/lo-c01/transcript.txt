Feedback that changes the next attempt



Human Services Supervisor: Can I ask you something about Tuesday? When I showed you the policy line next to your draft, how did that land?

Disability Services Program Consultant: Honestly, my stomach dropped at first. Then it helped. It was the first time anyone showed me exactly where the problem was.

Human Services Supervisor: That surprises me. I almost sent you a message saying it was a good start and needed tightening.

Disability Services Program Consultant: I would have changed a few words and sent it back. That is what happened with my last supervisor. Everything was fine, until it was not.

Human Services Supervisor: That is the trap. A warm message feels kind. But praise mostly turns attention to the person. In the largest review of feedback studies, feedback that included praise barely changed performance.

Disability Services Program Consultant: What actually changed my draft was seeing "should consider" next to the word "must." I could not argue with that. It was about the memo, not about me.

Human Services Supervisor: I also started by saying the guidance had to be exact and that I knew you could get it there. Did that matter, or did it sound like a script?

Disability Services Program Consultant: It mattered. Without it, I would have wondered whether you had decided I was not good at this. With it, the criticism felt like a reason to work harder.

Human Services Supervisor: That matches the research. In classroom experiments, one sentence naming high standards and belief in the student changed who revised their work. It mattered most for students who had the least reason to trust.

Disability Services Program Consultant: One thing I would change. In the meeting I just said okay. I needed time. The written summary and the Thursday check were what made it work.

Human Services Supervisor: That is useful. I took your okay as agreement. Next time I will offer the written version and a check before I assume anything.

Disability Services Program Consultant: And thanks for asking me this. It makes it easier to bring you a problem before it reaches the counties.
