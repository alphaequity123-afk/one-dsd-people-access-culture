# Core principles of One DHS People, Access and Culture

Adopted by the owner on 2026-11-15: the unified app follows the **People, Access and Culture Program Requirements
Document (2026-11-09)** as its core principles, and the **One DSD home page**
(https://one-people-access-culture.up.railway.app/home) as the model for its landing page. Where a later owner
ruling refines a point, the ruling is noted beside it.

## The promises (firm requirements on every page, file and answer)

1. **No sign-in** to read, learn or use the tools.
2. **Learning is your own.** No record of who visits, what they read, which lessons they take or how they answer.
   Learning activity is never counted, stored or shown, not even as anonymous totals, and never turned into evidence
   of compliance. (Branching scenarios keep the path only on the open page.)
3. **Your work stays with you.** Saved resources, practice plans, your place in a course, your job family and area
   choices stay on your own computer. The program keeps no copy.
4. **Private information stays out.** Every tool that accepts typed text reminds you to leave out client, case,
   health and personnel details.
5. **Facts have sources.** Every fact names where it came from; every resource links to its source.
6. **Plain language** for a busy professional, names spelled out.
7. **People decide.** Tools and answers support decisions; they never replace the person or area responsible.
8. **Open to everyone.** Keyboard, screen reader, magnification and phone; videos with captions and transcripts;
   every page prints in full.
9. **Easy to share.** Every resource, course and lesson has a link that opens exactly that item, and downloads as
   Word, Excel, PowerPoint, printable or plain text.

## How it works for people

- **Anything useful is no more than three selections away.**
- **Landing page** (kept short and calm, never slimmed further; owner rule 2026-11-15): the hero with its motion
  loop; a user guide button directly under the opening sentence; a task finder ("what are you working on?"); three
  doors (Learn, Apply a tool, Communities); the next three observances from the Culture Calendar; a direct entry
  point for supervisors, managers and directors.
- **Start**: three short questions (role, the work in front of you, time you have) suggest a first step and say why.
  Nothing chosen is saved or sent anywhere.
- **Every inner page works the same way**: what the page is for, the one to four things most people come to do,
  extra detail in panels that open when wanted; long catalogs one page at a time with search.
- **Four kinds of help**: Ask (answers from the program's own material with linked sources; current public research
  shown separately), Guidance (hints and worked examples during practice, keeps no record), Support (directory by
  need to the person or area responsible), and the user guide (welcome video and short topics).
- **Responses are evaluated; people never are.** No scores, no ranking of people, teams or beliefs.
- **Two purposes are equally real**: serving people well, and growing in understanding culture, including your own.

## What the program holds (One DHS, agency-wide)

Learning (more than 700 courses and resources under eight themes, the Core 15, the CLAS curriculum, connected series,
The Water We Swim In, deeper series, self-directed courses, 304 book summaries, leadership); Minnesota Communities
(42 briefs; Tribal Nations pages stand as written and point to the responsible Tribal liaison; no program training on
Tribal relations); the Culture Calendar (569 observances); practice (13 guided practices, reviews, practice cases,
planning and measurement, support for leaders: 89 questions behind nine decision doors, a directors' toolkit); the
four administrations' narrated introductions; nine areas of work; Understanding DHS reference; employee resource
groups; the equity policy in plain language. DSD is the deep example (program areas, work situations, equity plan,
Community Outreach and Engagement, policy workspace, Amplify Equity, Learning Lab).

## Owner rulings that refine the document

- No system or participation notices in visible text (no "participation is voluntary", "saved", "recorded"
  notices) — the promises are kept, not announced. (2026-11-15)
- "The person or area responsible", never "office". Never "AI" or "tutor"; the feature is Guidance.
- People, Access and Culture wording; DEI/DEIA terms replaced (official names excepted). MMB Leading with DEIA is held
  aside pending the owner's review.
- One application for the whole agency, built on the job families and competencies (data/workforce), with DSD as the
  model and the administrations layer; nothing hidden by job family or area.
- Trainings: all-staff and job-family versions from the corpus, narrated by the recurring cast (no names), each with a
  photorealistic 4K hero image.
