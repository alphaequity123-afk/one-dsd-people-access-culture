# Video script: Sustaining Intercultural Learning through Coaching and Reflection

- **Status:** Script only, written for the recurring cast (docs/media-scripts/CAST.md). Not recorded.
- **Publish on:** /learning/self-directed/index.html?course=A04
- **Length:** about 1.5 minutes (about 204 spoken words)
- **Cast:** Narrator (C3, Host voice), Case manager (C5, Host voice), Newer colleague (C7, Co-host voice) (see docs/media-scripts/CAST.md)
- **Accessibility:** captions and a full transcript are required before publishing (docs/ACCESSIBILITY-STANDARD.md). On-screen text must also be spoken. Describe any visual that carries meaning.

## Scene 1: The help we give each other (0:00–0:25)
**Visual:** A team workspace with neighboring desks. The course title appears on screen as the narrator (C3) speaks it.
**Narrator (C3):** Much of what we learn at work comes from a colleague who answers when we ask, and the kind of help they give shapes what we can learn next. Sustaining Intercultural Learning through Coaching and Reflection prepares you for the moment someone brings you their work, or their own uncertainty, and asks what you think.

## Scene 2: Feedback on a service note (0:25–0:55)
**Visual:** The newer colleague (C7) turns a monitor toward the case manager (C5). A draft service note is on screen; its wording is spoken in the scene.
**Newer colleague (C7):** Before I save this service note, would you read it? I wrote, “The person did not reply because they were uninterested.”
**Case manager (C5):** I see one contact attempt in the record here, and it wasn’t answered.
**Newer colleague (C7):** That’s right. I tried to word it respectfully, and I’d really like your honest feedback.
**Case manager (C5):** Thank you for asking me. Give me a moment to think about what feedback would be most useful to you here.
**Visual:** The case manager (C5) reads the note again. The scene ends before any feedback is given.

## Closing (0:55–1:25)
**Visual:** The narrator (C3) in the workspace. The closing question appears on screen as the narrator speaks it.
**Narrator (C3):** The course gives you practice agreeing on a meaningful learning purpose, along with a voluntary choice about how to reflect. You will give feedback about a specific practice rather than a presumed developmental level, compare the support people receive at onboarding, development, and transition, and describe a change in understanding with a fitting next question. Here is one to start with, and your answer can stay private: what is one first interpretation of yours that another meaning has widened?

## Transcript notes
A short introduction: a newer colleague asks a case manager to read a service note that explains an unanswered contact as lack of interest, and the case manager pauses to decide what feedback would help.
