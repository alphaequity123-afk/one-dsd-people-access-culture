# Self-directed course video scripts

One short (about 1.5 minute) introduction for each of the 72 self-directed courses: 24 Foundation (F01 to F24), 24 Intermediate (I01 to I24) and 24 Advanced (A01 to A24). Each is named by its course number and goes on that course's page, `/learning/self-directed/index.html?course=<number>`. Each has three scenes: why the course matters, a short moment at work that stops at the decision, and what the course helps you do, with a question to carry in.

Titles match `lib/content/self-directed-data.json` on November 24, 2026. Speakers are identified by role and played by the recurring cast; `docs/media-scripts/CAST.md` lists which cast member and voice takes each role. Nothing here is recorded or published.
