# Video script: Fair Feedback, Retention, and Transitions

- **Status:** Script only, written for the recurring cast (docs/media-scripts/CAST.md). Not recorded.
- **Publish on:** /learning/self-directed/index.html?course=I20
- **Length:** about 1.6 minutes (about 218 spoken words)
- **Cast:** Narrator (C3, Host voice), Case manager (C5, Host voice), Community partner (C1, Co-host voice) (see docs/media-scripts/CAST.md)
- **Accessibility:** captions and a full transcript are required before publishing (docs/ACCESSIBILITY-STANDARD.md). On-screen text must also be spoken. Describe any visual that carries meaning. The only on-screen text is the course title, which the narrator speaks; documents and screens shown in the video carry no legible text.

## Scene 1: Serious words, nothing to act on (0:00–0:15)
**Visual:** The narrator (C3) at a desk with a closed review folder. A title card shows the course title.
**Narrator (C3):** Feedback can sound serious and still leave someone with nothing specific to act on. Fair Feedback, Retention, and Transitions prepares you for writing feedback and reviews that stay with the work, whether the review concerns a colleague or a provider.

## Scene 2: A word in the review (0:15–0:55)
**Visual:** The case manager (C5) and the community partner (C1), a county partner, at a table with a practice service review open on a laptop; the screen text is not legible.
**Case manager (C5):** This provider's responses have been late several times now. I'm about to write in the review that they seem uncommitted.
**Community partner (C1):** The delays have been hard on our side too. That word will carry a lot of weight in a review, though.
**Case manager (C5):** That's partly the point. The concern is real, and I want it to sound serious.
**Community partner (C1):** Around the same time, we changed how instructions reach them. As far as I know, nobody has gone back over that handoff.
**Case manager (C5):** So I'm deciding what this review should say, and I'm not sure I'm there yet.
**Visual:** The cursor rests in the review field. No wording is shown.

## Closing (0:55–1:35)
**Visual:** The narrator (C3) in the same office setting.
**Narrator (C3):** The course gives you practice with specific feedback that focuses on the work and the conditions around it rather than on personality or identity. It also looks at access to assignments, support and useful feedback across a review period, listening to reasons for staying or leaving without assigning a shared group experience, and support that can be lost when a role or supervisor changes. As you start, consider what you could point to in your next written concern that the other person could also see.

## Transcript notes
Summary for the media manifest: A short introduction: a case manager drafting a practice service review is about to call a provider uncommitted after delayed responses, and the narrator describes what the course offers for fair feedback, retention and transitions.
