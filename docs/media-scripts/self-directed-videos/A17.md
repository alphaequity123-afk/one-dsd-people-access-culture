# Video script: Opportunity across the Employee Life Cycle

- **Status:** Script only, written for the recurring cast (docs/media-scripts/CAST.md). Not recorded.
- **Publish on:** /learning/self-directed/index.html?course=A17
- **Length:** about 1.5 minutes (about 226 spoken words)
- **Cast:** Narrator (C3, Host voice), Case manager (C5, Host voice), Supervisor (C6, Co-host voice) (see docs/media-scripts/CAST.md)
- **Accessibility:** captions and a full transcript are required before publishing (docs/ACCESSIBILITY-STANDARD.md). On-screen text must also be spoken. Describe any visual that carries meaning.

## Scene 1: The same few people (0:00–0:25)
**Visual:** A whiteboard assignment grid in which the same three sticky notes appear in every row. The course title appears on screen and is spoken. No real logos or real people.
**Narrator (C3):** Some opportunities seem to go to the same few people and it is not always clear why. Opportunity across the Employee Life Cycle is an advanced self-directed course for the moment you look at how access to one opportunity shapes the ones that come later, from entering the work to developing within it.

## Scene 2: Asking to be considered (0:25–1:05)
**Visual:** The case manager (C5) stops by the supervisor's (C6) office. A printed assignment posting lies on the desk; its text is not readable.
**Case manager (C5):** I saw the developmental service-review assignment come up again. I'd like to be considered. I've never led one, though.
**Supervisor (C6):** I'd like that too. The trouble is that the posting only accepts people who have already led that review.
**Case manager (C5):** Then how does anyone new ever get the experience?
**Supervisor (C6):** Right now, there's no described way to prepare for it. It has gone to the same group for a while.
**Case manager (C5):** I'm not saying they don't deserve it.
**Supervisor (C6):** I know. I'm not sure yet what to tell you, so let me work out what to ask first.

## Closing (1:05–1:40)
**Visual:** The course title card, then the first slide of the course.
**Narrator (C3):** The course helps you trace how access to one opportunity affects later ones, distinguish job-relevant criteria from informal familiarity and sponsorship, compare ways to make development opportunities more visible and accessible, and prepare a recommendation that connects workforce conditions with the quality of public service. Employment decisions themselves stay with their responsible processes. As you begin, consider an opportunity you know: which requirement describes what a person needs to start, and which describes what the opportunity is meant to develop?

## Transcript notes
Media manifest summary: A short introduction to Opportunity across the Employee Life Cycle: a case manager asks to join a review assignment open only to people who have already led it, and a supervisor weighs what to examine.
