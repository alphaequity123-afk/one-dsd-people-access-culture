- **Length:** about 1.5 minutes (about 219 spoken words)

## Scene 1: An invitation that sounds generous (0:00–0:20)
**Visual:** An empty meeting table with a stack of printed invitations. The course title appears on screen and is spoken. No real logos or real people.
**Narrator (C3):** Asking people to help shape a service is easy to say, and much harder to describe honestly. Community Partnership that Can Influence a Decision is an advanced self-directed course for the moment you invite community knowledge into your work and need to say plainly what that knowledge can still change.

## Scene 2: Before the flyer goes out (0:20–1:00)
**Visual:** The program manager (C2) and the newer colleague (C7) at a desk, looking at a draft flyer for a meeting about a service introduction. The headline is the line the newer colleague reads aloud.
**Newer colleague (C7):** I finished the flyer for the service introduction meeting. The headline says, "Design our service introduction together." I think that will bring more people in.
**Program manager (C2):** It might. Before we send it, though, let's be clear with ourselves about what's still open. The service route is settled, and so is the explanation.
**Newer colleague (C7):** So what can people actually change when they come?
**Program manager (C2):** Right now, the photograph. That's about it.
**Newer colleague (C7):** That's a lot smaller than the headline makes it sound.
**Program manager (C2):** It is. So we have a decision to make about this invitation before it goes anywhere.

## Closing (1:00–1:35)
**Visual:** The course title card, then the first slide of the course.
**Narrator (C3):** The course helps you identify which choices remain open to community influence and which responsibilities stay fixed. You'll compare participation designs for access, influence, and burden, consider how expertise, support, and compensation questions affect who can take part, and prepare a truthful account of how input will be considered and returned. As you begin, consider this: when people give you their time and knowledge, what would they need to hear afterward to know what it did?

Media manifest summary: A short introduction to Community Partnership that Can Influence a Decision: a team drafting a "design together" invitation realizes only a photograph is still open, and must decide what to promise.
