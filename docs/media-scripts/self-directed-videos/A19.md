# Video script: A Learning Culture that Extends Staff Capability

- **Status:** Script only, written for the recurring cast (docs/media-scripts/CAST.md). Not recorded.
- **Publish on:** /learning/self-directed/index.html?course=A19
- **Length:** about 1.5 minutes (about 221 spoken words)
- **Cast:** Narrator (C3, Host voice), Newer colleague (C7, Co-host voice), Program manager (C2, Host voice) (see docs/media-scripts/CAST.md)
- **Accessibility:** captions and a full transcript are required before publishing (docs/ACCESSIBILITY-STANDARD.md). On-screen text must also be spoken. Describe any visual that carries meaning.

## Scene 1: A generous collection (0:00–0:25)
**Visual:** A shared folder on screen, holding dozens of learning files with unreadable names. The course title appears and is spoken. No real logos or real people.
**Narrator (C3):** A large learning collection can be generous and still leave someone unsure where to begin. A Learning Culture that Extends Staff Capability is an advanced self-directed course for the moment you choose, build, or recommend a way for staff to learn, and need it to fit a real purpose and the people who will use it.

## Scene 2: The night before an explanation (0:25–1:00)
**Visual:** The newer colleague (C7) and the program manager (C2) at a laptop showing the folder.
**Newer colleague (C7):** Tomorrow I have to explain a service next step to someone, and I wanted to prepare. I opened the learning collection, and honestly, I couldn't tell where to start.
**Program manager (C2):** You're not the first person to say that. The team's plan is to send another announcement reminding everyone the collection is there.
**Newer colleague (C7):** I already know it's there. That isn't really my problem.
**Program manager (C2):** Then we should understand what the problem is before anything goes out.
**Newer colleague (C7):** Where would you even begin?
**Program manager (C2):** That's what I'm asking myself right now.

## Closing (1:00–1:35)
**Visual:** The course title card, then the first slide of the course.
**Narrator (C3):** The course helps you compare learning options against a meaningful purpose and the needs of the learners. You'll connect understanding, practice, feedback, and a considered next step, identify barriers to learning across the employee life cycle, and explain how shared resource choices support personal growth and practical equity work. Personal reflection in the course stays private. As you begin, think about this: when feedback has helped you rethink an answer, what did it give you that "good job" would not?
