# Video script: Using Culture Briefs to Prepare, Listen, and Learn

- **Status:** Script only, written for the recurring cast (docs/media-scripts/CAST.md). Not recorded.
- **Publish on:** /learning/self-directed/index.html?course=I13
- **Length:** about 1.6 minutes (about 226 spoken words)
- **Cast:** Narrator (C3, Host voice), Case manager (C5, Host voice), Community partner (C1, Co-host voice) (see docs/media-scripts/CAST.md)
- **Accessibility:** captions and a full transcript are required before publishing (docs/ACCESSIBILITY-STANDARD.md). On-screen text must also be spoken. Describe any visual that carries meaning. The only on-screen text is the course title, which the narrator speaks; documents and screens shown in the video carry no legible text.

## Scene 1: Preparing before a conversation (0:00–0:20)
**Visual:** The narrator (C3) at a desk with a printed community brief whose pages carry no legible text. A title card shows the course title.
**Narrator (C3):** Before outreach or a first meeting, many of us read a community brief to get our bearings. Using Culture Briefs to Prepare, Listen, and Learn is about what comes next, when that preparation meets a real person who may want something the brief never mentioned.

## Scene 2: A partner and a later update (0:20–1:05)
**Visual:** The case manager (C5) and the community partner (C1) on a video call, each in a plain office.
**Community partner (C1):** I'm glad the person found their way to you. Our organization helped them get started, so we're happy to keep receiving the updates and passing them along, the way we did at the beginning.
**Case manager (C5):** Your help made that first contact possible, and the route has been easy for everyone. On our last call, though, they asked to hear from me directly about their service.
**Community partner (C1):** We know them well. Honestly, it might be simpler for all of us if nothing changes.
**Case manager (C5):** Our team has been leaning the same way, since it's already set up. The next update is ready to go, and I have to decide who it goes to.
**Visual:** The picture holds on the case manager (C5), who has not yet answered. No decision is shown.

## Closing (1:05–1:35)
**Visual:** The narrator (C3) in the same office setting.
**Narrator (C3):** The course helps you use a brief's history, institutions and perspectives to shape thoughtful questions, tell a contextual generalization from a claim about an individual, seek varied perspectives without asking one partner to represent a whole community, and revise an invitation after hearing what people actually say. As you begin, consider what you would want to know before an arrangement that helped at the start becomes the rule for every later contact.
