# Video script: Fair Recruitment and Selection Through Relevant Evidence

- **Status:** Script only, written for the recurring cast (docs/media-scripts/CAST.md). Not recorded.
- **Publish on:** /learning/self-directed/index.html?course=I17
- **Length:** about 1.5 minutes (about 210 spoken words)
- **Cast:** Narrator (C3, Host voice), Supervisor (C6, Co-host voice), Case manager (C5, Host voice) (see docs/media-scripts/CAST.md)
- **Accessibility:** captions and a full transcript are required before publishing (docs/ACCESSIBILITY-STANDARD.md). On-screen text must also be spoken. Describe any visual that carries meaning. The only on-screen text is the course title, which the narrator speaks; documents and screens shown in the video carry no legible text.

## Scene 1: After the interview (0:00–0:15)
**Visual:** The narrator (C3) in an empty conference room with closed folders on the table. A title card shows the course title.
**Narrator (C3):** Hiring panels often end with a quick conversation about who stood out. Fair Recruitment and Selection Through Relevant Evidence prepares you for that conversation, when a strong impression and the approved criteria begin pulling in different directions.

## Scene 2: A familiar applicant (0:15–0:55)
**Visual:** The supervisor (C6) and the case manager (C5) at the conference table after an interview, rating sheets face down.
**Supervisor (C6):** That last applicant was great. I worked with them on a project a while back, and they handled every question without hesitating.
**Case manager (C5):** They were confident, no question. It was an easy interview to sit through.
**Supervisor (C6):** In this role, confidence goes a long way. I'd be comfortable giving that a lot of weight when we rate.
**Case manager (C5):** Our rating sheet for this question is about analytical reasoning, and I wrote notes on each answer. I'm trying to work out how today's impression fits with that.
**Supervisor (C6):** Then let's settle how we're rating before we turn these over.
**Visual:** Both reach for the rating sheets. No rating is shown.

## Closing (0:55–1:30)
**Visual:** The narrator (C3) in the same conference room.
**Narrator (C3):** The course helps you tell apart the capabilities a job truly requires from credentials or preferences that simply feel familiar. It looks at who can find and use an opportunity, how to compare candidates with stable criteria and relevant evidence, and how to name a possible selection barrier while actual hiring decisions stay with the approved hiring process. As you begin, consider what part of the work you are describing when you say someone seems like a good fit.

Summary for the media manifest: A short introduction: two panel members weigh a familiar applicant's confidence against the approved criterion before rating, and the narrator describes what the course offers for fair recruitment and selection through relevant evidence.
