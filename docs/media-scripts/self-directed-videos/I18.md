# Video script: Onboarding That Makes the Work and the Culture Understandable

- **Status:** Script only, written for the recurring cast (docs/media-scripts/CAST.md). Not recorded.
- **Publish on:** /learning/self-directed/index.html?course=I18
- **Length:** about 1.5 minutes (about 212 spoken words)
- **Cast:** Narrator (C3, Host voice), Program manager (C2, Host voice), Newer colleague (C7, Co-host voice) (see docs/media-scripts/CAST.md)
- **Accessibility:** captions and a full transcript are required before publishing (docs/ACCESSIBILITY-STANDARD.md). On-screen text must also be spoken. Describe any visual that carries meaning. The only on-screen text is the course title, which the narrator speaks; documents and screens shown in the video carry no legible text.

## Scene 1: Steps nobody writes down (0:00–0:20)
**Visual:** The narrator (C3) beside a printed onboarding guide with no legible text. A title card shows the course title.
**Narrator (C3):** Every team has a few steps that nobody writes down because everyone already knows them. Onboarding That Makes the Work and the Culture Understandable prepares you for a new colleague's first weeks, when the formal expectations and the unwritten ones both shape how their work is seen.

## Scene 2: The review nobody mentioned (0:20–1:00)
**Visual:** The program manager (C2) at the newer colleague's (C7) desk; a printed first draft lies between them, its text not legible.
**Program manager (C2):** Your draft went up for sign-off yesterday. It skipped the review the team does together on Thursdays.
**Newer colleague (C7):** I didn't know there was a Thursday review. The guide says the team will pick it up, so I thought sending it on was the next step.
**Program manager (C2):** Most of us settle draft questions in that conversation. Everyone else seemed to know about it.
**Newer colleague (C7):** Is there somewhere I should have looked?
**Program manager (C2):** I'm writing up early feedback for our supervisor this week, and I need to decide what to say about this.
**Visual:** The newer colleague (C7) waits for an answer. No feedback is shown.

## Closing (1:00–1:30)
**Visual:** The narrator (C3) in the same office setting.
**Narrator (C3):** The course helps you explain both the formal work expectations and the unwritten norms of your team without making newcomers guess. It covers making information, access support and relationships usable from the start, avoiding the assignment of cultural education or representation to a new colleague, and connecting early feedback with actual work evidence and chances to learn. As you start, consider which of your team's everyday steps a capable newcomer would have no way to discover alone.

## Transcript notes
Summary for the media manifest: A short introduction: a new colleague learns their draft skipped a review step known only through informal conversation, and the narrator describes what the course offers for onboarding that makes the work and the culture understandable.
