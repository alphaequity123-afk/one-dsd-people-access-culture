## Closing: What this course covers (2:27–3:33)
**Visual:** A plain title card that reads "Accessibility, Disability Justice, and Organizational Culture." The narrator reads it aloud. The card changes to a simple list of four topics, each read aloud as it appears.

**{{NARRATOR}}:** Accessibility, Disability Justice, and Organizational Culture. Two moments in one afternoon, and the same question underneath: who is this work designed for? This course applies the culture lens from Module 3 to access. You will look at how treating writing as the only route can shut people out, and how plain language, alternative formats and documented spoken input can widen the way in. You will separate the goal of a standard from the one method it requires, using Universal Design and Universal Design for Learning. You will consider why a culture of perfectionism can make asking for an accommodation feel risky, and what lowers the cost of asking. And you will learn from Sins Invalid's principles of disability justice and practice collective access in DSD meetings and engagement. Keep both moments in mind as you go. What would you change first?
