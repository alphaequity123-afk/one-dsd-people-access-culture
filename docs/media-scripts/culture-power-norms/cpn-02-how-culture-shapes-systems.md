## Scene 4: What the course covers (2:15–3:05)
**Visual:** The room fades to an illustration of a single notice moving along a path. Along the path, four signposts appear and are read aloud by the narrator: "Rule," "Form," "Timeline," "Resources."
**{{NARRATOR}}:** Habits don't stay habits. Over time they become rules, forms, timelines and budgets that keep working long after anyone remembers why. In this course you'll practice explaining a recurring problem as events, as a pattern and as a structure, an approach drawn from Peter Senge's work on learning organizations. You'll trace defaults back to where they came from and who owns them. You'll use john a. powell's idea that people are situated differently within the same structures, along with targeted universalism, which keeps one goal for everyone and designs different paths to reach it. And you'll look at where in a system a change makes the most difference, and how to tell whether it helped.
