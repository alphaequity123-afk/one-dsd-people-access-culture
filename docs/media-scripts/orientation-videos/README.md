# Orientation video scripts

Short (about 2 to 3 minute) introductions for large groups of courses that have no video, modeled on the diversity series level videos.

- `catalog-overview.md` and nine `catalog-*.md` scripts, one per pathway of the People, Access and Culture course catalog.
- Seven `series-*.md` scripts for the authored series: Disability Inclusion, Intercultural Practice and Equity, DSD Service System Practice, Supervisor and Manager, Workforce and Leadership, Program Integrity, and Emotions and Values.

Each script names the page(s) to publish on, its length, its cast and its accessibility needs. Speakers are identified by role and played by the recurring cast; `docs/media-scripts/CAST.md` lists which cast member and voice takes each role. Spoken course counts come from the repository on November 24, 2026; check them again before recording. Nothing here is recorded or published.
