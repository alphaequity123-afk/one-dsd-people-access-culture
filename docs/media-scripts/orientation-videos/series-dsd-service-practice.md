# Video script: Getting started with DSD Service System Practice

- **Status:** Script only, written for the recurring cast (docs/media-scripts/CAST.md). Not recorded.
- **Publish on:** /courses/dsd-01-minnesota-service-system (first course in the series), /learn/collections/services-choice-system (the staff collection "Services, choice and the DSD system," which opens with that course)
- **Length:** about 3 minutes (about 370 spoken words)
- **Cast:** Narrator (C3, Host voice), Case manager (C5, Host voice), Community partner (C1, Co-host voice) (see docs/media-scripts/CAST.md)
- **Accessibility:** captions and a full transcript are required before publishing (docs/ACCESSIBILITY-STANDARD.md). On-screen text must also be spoken. Describe any visual that carries meaning.

## Scene 1: What the series is for (0:00–0:32)
**Visual:** A simple diagram of a person at the center, with lines to a case manager, a county, a provider, an employer and housing. Title card reads "DSD Service System Practice." No real logos or real people.
**Narrator (C3):** This is the DSD Service System Practice series. It is for staff in the Disability Services Division and the colleagues who work alongside it: program and policy staff, case managers and care coordinators, lead agency and provider oversight staff, quality and data staff, and supervisors and leaders. The series explains how home and community-based services connect, and where a person may need help moving from one step to the next.

## Scene 2: A moment at work (0:32–1:05)
**Visual:** A case manager on a video call with a county partner, a service plan open beside the call window.
**Community partner (C1):** The person we talked about last week asked me again who to call about housing. I gave them your number, but I wasn't sure that was right.
**Case manager (C5):** It's right for the service plan. For housing, there may be someone else who should be involved.
**Community partner (C1):** I'd rather not send them to a third person who sends them back.
**Case manager (C5):** Neither would I. Let me find out today and call you both, so they hear one answer.

## Scene 3: How the series is organized (1:05–1:42)
**Visual:** Three labeled bands appear one at a time: "Foundations," "Practice," "Leadership." Each label is read aloud as it appears.
**Narrator (C3):** The series has 19 courses in three groups. Foundations, six courses, covers the Minnesota service system, service coordination and navigation, self-direction and independent living, personal outcomes, competitive integrated employment, and benefits and economic stability. Practice, twelve courses, goes deeper into situations case managers and program staff meet often, including brain injury, aging and health, communication methods, positive supports, mental health, co-occurring substance use, housing instability, and justice involvement and reentry. Leadership, one course, is about leading system change with counties, Tribal Nations and providers.

## Scene 4: Choosing a starting point (1:42–2:27)
**Visual:** A course page showing a Minnesota example, a sorting exercise, a scenario with feedback, and a review tool a learner can run on their own work.
**Narrator (C3):** If the program names and responsibilities are new to you, start with Foundations of Disability Services in Minnesota. If you already know the system, choose the Practice course closest to the people you work with. Each course takes about 45 to 55 minutes across four or five short lessons you can take in any order, with Minnesota examples, practice with feedback, knowledge checks and a job aid or review you can use on your own work. Keep any reflection notes private, without naming the people you serve. Your progress is saved in your browser, with no score, ranking or completion requirement.

## Closing (2:27–2:47)
**Visual:** The first course card, with its title read aloud: "Foundations of Disability Services in Minnesota."
**Narrator (C3):** The courses build a map you can return to, but they do not replace current program guidance. For an actual eligibility or service decision, check that guidance and the responsible office. Participation is voluntary. Begin wherever the work in front of you points.

## Transcript notes
Media manifest summary: An introduction to the 19-course DSD Service System Practice series: who it serves, its Foundations, Practice and Leadership groups, how to choose a starting point, and how the courses work.
