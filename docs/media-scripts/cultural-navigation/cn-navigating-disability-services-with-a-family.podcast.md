## The stack of papers

**Host (female):** A parent hands a navigator a stack of papers and says, I don't know what any of this is. Where do you start?

**Co-host (male):** Not with all of it, I hope.

**Host (female):** Right. Find the one paper that matters now, explain it and write down the next step. The whole path has five parts: first contact, assessment, planning and choice, services starting, and renewals and changes. But a family only needs the next step today.


## The assessment

**Co-host (male):** Who does the assessment in Minnesota?

**Host (female):** Lead agencies: counties, tribal nations and managed care organizations, using the state's MnCHOICES system and certified assessors. A navigator doesn't assess. A navigator helps the family get ready.

**Co-host (male):** Some families want to show they're doing fine, so nobody thinks they can't cope.

**Host (female):** That's very human. The navigator can explain that the assessment is how the agency learns what help might make life easier, and talk through a real week with them, hard nights included. Then the family decides what to share, in their own words. Preparing, never scripting.


## Choosing

**Co-host (male):** Then they get a list of providers and a deadline.

**Host (female):** And this is where the law helps. For people on waivers, Minnesota's informed decision-making process includes things like all available options, cultural needs, visits or hands-on experiences, talking with peers and enough time to decide. A family who knows they can ask for a visit is in a much stronger position.


## After approval

**Co-host (male):** And once services are approved, it's done?

**Host (female):** Not quite. That's when things quietly stall. Help the family know what should happen next, follow up with the case manager when they agree, read every letter together for dates and appeal rights, and keep a simple record.

**Co-host (male):** And free help along the way?

**Host (female):** Disability Hub MN is a free statewide resource. For health coverage, MNsure navigators help for free too. A navigator doesn't have to know everything. They have to know where to go.
