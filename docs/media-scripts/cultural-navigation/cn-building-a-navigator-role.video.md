# Video script: Just Come Along

- **Status:** Script only, written for the recurring cast. Not recorded.
- **Publish on:** /courses/cn-building-a-navigator-role (Building a Cultural Navigator Role)
- **Length:** about 2 minutes
- **Voices:** Each character uses their approved named reference from `config/character-voices.json`; see `docs/media-scripts/CAST.md`.
- **Accessibility:** captions and a full transcript are required before publishing (docs/ACCESSIBILITY-STANDARD.md). On-screen text must also be spoken.

**Cast:**

- **Narrator:** C3 (Kelly voice)
- **Program manager:** C2 (LaToya voice)
- **Supervisor:** C6 (Robert voice)
- **Case manager:** C5 (Susan voice)
- **People served:** described or off camera, so no cast member is placed in a particular community.

**Scene 1. A meeting room. The program manager and the supervisor.**

PROGRAM MANAGER: The navigator funding was cut. Could we ask our bilingual staff to just come along on a few visits?

SUPERVISOR: That's navigation without the job. Nobody would count it, and it lands on two or three people.

**Scene 2. The supervisor and the case manager, privately.**

SUPERVISOR: People keep asking you to help families in your language, on top of your caseload. How much is it, really?

CASE MANAGER: Most days. I don't mind helping. I mind that it's invisible.

**Scene 3. The meeting room, later.**

SUPERVISOR: Three options. A partnership with a community organization. Recognizing the work in the case manager's role and caseload. Or a smaller, funded navigator role focused on assessments.

PROGRAM MANAGER: And a scope that says what the role is not.

NARRATOR: Start with what the role is for. Write the scope. Pay for it. Support it. A role, not a favor.
