# Video script: One Paper at a Time

- **Status:** Script only, written for the recurring cast. Not recorded.
- **Publish on:** /courses/cn-navigating-disability-services-with-a-family (Navigating Disability Services with a Family)
- **Length:** about 2 minutes
- **Voices:** Each character uses their approved named reference from `config/character-voices.json`; see `docs/media-scripts/CAST.md`.
- **Accessibility:** captions and a full transcript are required before publishing (docs/ACCESSIBILITY-STANDARD.md). On-screen text must also be spoken.

**Cast:**

- **Narrator:** C3 (Kelly voice)
- **Navigator:** C1 (Brian voice)
- **Peer supporter:** C4 (John voice)
- **People served:** described or off camera, so no cast member is placed in a particular community.

**Scene 1. A kitchen table. The navigator sits beside a parent, who is off camera. A stack of papers.**

NARRATOR: A referral, a letter, a form and a lot of new words, all at once.

NAVIGATOR: Let's not do all of these today. This one is the important one. It's about setting up the assessment. The next step is choosing a date. Let's write that down.

**Scene 2. The same table, a week later. The peer supporter has joined.**

PEER SUPPORTER: When I had my first assessment, I wanted to look like I had it all handled. I left out the hard nights.

NAVIGATOR: The assessment is how they learn what help might make things easier. Do you want to talk through a real week, nights too? Then you decide what to share.

**Scene 3. Later. A provider list on the table.**

NAVIGATOR: You can ask to visit before you choose, and to talk with someone who chose differently. And let's ask what the Friday deadline is for.

NARRATOR: Prepare, never script. One step at a time. And keep a record, so the family stays in charge of their own case.
