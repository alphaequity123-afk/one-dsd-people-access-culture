# Video script: None Identified

- **Status:** Script only, written for the recurring cast. Not recorded.
- **Publish on:** /courses/cn-culture-in-informed-choice (Culture in Informed Choice)
- **Length:** about 2 minutes
- **Voices:** Each character uses their approved named reference from `config/character-voices.json`; see `docs/media-scripts/CAST.md`.
- **Accessibility:** captions and a full transcript are required before publishing (docs/ACCESSIBILITY-STANDARD.md). On-screen text must also be spoken.

**Cast:**

- **Narrator:** C3 (Kelly voice)
- **Assessor:** C8 (Noor voice)
- **Case manager:** C5 (Susan voice)
- **Newer colleague:** C7 (Takoda voice)
- **People served:** described or off camera, so no cast member is placed in a particular community.

**Scene 1. An office. The assessor scrolls through plans.**

NARRATOR: Cultural needs: none identified. Again, and again.

ASSESSOR: (to the case manager) Do we ever actually ask?

CASE MANAGER: Not really. It feels awkward.

**Scene 2. The newer colleague's desk.**

NEWER COLLEAGUE: The family's from a community where women always handle care, so I'll note the mother as main helper.

CASE MANAGER: Let's ask them instead. Who helps, and who do they want with them when decisions are made? Every family is different.

**Scene 3. A planning meeting.**

ASSESSOR: What's important to you in how people help you at home? Anything about your faith, food or routines helpers should know?

NARRATOR: The answer goes into the plan in the person's own words: their sister present for decisions, a female helper for bathing, time for prayer before noon.

NARRATOR: Minnesota law makes cultural needs part of informed decision making. Ask, don't assume. Record it specifically. Ask again when life changes.
