# Video script: After Hours

- **Status:** Script only, written for the recurring cast. Not recorded.
- **Publish on:** /courses/cn-sustaining-the-navigator (Sustaining the Navigator)
- **Length:** about 2 minutes
- **Voices:** women in the cast use the female Host voice (`host1`), men use the male Co-host voice (`geffen`).
- **Accessibility:** captions and a full transcript are required before publishing (docs/ACCESSIBILITY-STANDARD.md). On-screen text must also be spoken.

**Cast:**

- **Narrator:** C3 (Host voice)
- **Navigator:** C1 (Co-host voice)
- **Supervisor:** C6 (Co-host voice)
- **People served:** described or off camera, so no cast member is placed in a particular community.

**Scene 1. Night. The navigator's phone lights up again.**

NARRATOR: The third call tonight. A family in their own community, afraid of a letter.

**Scene 2. The supervisor's office, the next morning.**

SUPERVISOR: You've seemed quieter lately, and I know your caseload nearly doubled. How are you carrying all this?

NAVIGATOR: Honestly? I dread my phone.

SUPERVISOR: Let's look at the caseload together. And let's set hours, an after-hours message and where urgent calls should go.

**Scene 3. Evening. The navigator answers a call.**

NAVIGATOR: I want to help with this. I'm off now, so let's talk tomorrow at nine. If it's an emergency, call this number.

NARRATOR: Hearing hard stories has a cost. Organizations carry most of the answer: caseloads, schedules, training and supervision. A kind boundary protects the work, and the person doing it.
