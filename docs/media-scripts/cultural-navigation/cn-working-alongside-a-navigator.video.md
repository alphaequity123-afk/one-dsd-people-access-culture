# Video script: The Briefing

- **Status:** Script only, written for the recurring cast. Not recorded.
- **Publish on:** /courses/cn-working-alongside-a-navigator (Working Alongside a Cultural Navigator)
- **Length:** about 2 minutes
- **Voices:** Each character uses their approved named reference from `config/character-voices.json`; see `docs/media-scripts/CAST.md`.
- **Accessibility:** captions and a full transcript are required before publishing (docs/ACCESSIBILITY-STANDARD.md). On-screen text must also be spoken.

**Cast:**

- **Narrator:** C3 (Kelly voice)
- **Case manager:** C5 (Susan voice)
- **Navigator:** C1 (Brian voice)
- **Assessor:** C8 (Noor voice)
- **People served:** described or off camera, so no cast member is placed in a particular community.

**Scene 1. The case manager's desk. A phone call.**

NARRATOR: A planning meeting is next week. The person asked for a navigator from a community organization to come.

NAVIGATOR: (on the phone) Could you send me the whole file, so I'm ready?

CASE MANAGER: I'd like you to be ready too. Let me check that the release is signed. Then I'll send what the meeting needs: what it's for, the decisions that might come up and who's interpreting.

NAVIGATOR: That works. Who explains the options?

CASE MANAGER: I will. The interpreter carries the words. You help the family follow along and ask what they need to ask. The assessor records the decision.

**Scene 2. The meeting, a week later.**

ASSESSOR: Can you tell me about a usual morning?

NAVIGATOR: (starting to answer) Well, most mornings...

ASSESSOR: (gently) Thank you, that helps. I'd love to hear it from you too. (turning to the person, off camera)

NARRATOR: The navigator smiles and sits back. Their job is to help the person speak, not to speak for them.

**Scene 3. After the meeting.**

CASE MANAGER: Before you go, can you tell me in your own words what happens next?

NARRATOR: One last step: a plain-language note to the family, and an update to the navigator, with the family's agreement.

NARRATOR: Consent first. Clear roles. The person at the center. Close the loop.
